Our Mission

Democratising fashion expertise

MUSE was built on a simple belief: that expert fashion advice should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford a personal stylist. Great dressing is a skill that can be learned — and the first step is understanding colour.

We built a sophisticated colour theory engine that analyses your outfit at the pixel level, maps it against your skin tone, and delivers the kind of advice a trained fashion consultant would give — instantly, privately, and for free.

Everything runs on your own server. No data leaves your machine. No API keys required. Just pure fashion intelligence, open and transparent.

The Engine

How the intelligence works

01

Pixel Sampling

Jimp reads your image at 80×80 resolution and samples every pixel's RGB value for colour data.

02

Colour Classification

03
📐
Style Inference

Pattern-matching rules detect style categories — Earth Tone, Bold & Vivid, Classic, Pastel and more.

04
⚖️

Design Philosophy

Principles we build by

Colour is Personal

No two people have the same complexion. Every recommendation in MUSE is filtered through ten precision skin tone profiles, because a colour that looks magnificent on one person can be unflattering on another.

Less is More Powerful

A well-edited outfit of two or three considered colours always outperforms a busy arrangement of many. MUSE rewards restraint and penalises colour overload in its scoring algorithm.

Context Defines Style

The same garment can be perfect or inappropriate depending on occasion. MUSE maps every outfit to the right setting — office, casual, evening, festival — so recommendations always make sense in context.
